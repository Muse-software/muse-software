| Pillar | Share of the week | Should it have been |
|---|---|---|
| Ventures | | |
| Marketing | | |

The rule says client work wins when it exists, and Ventures and Marketing fill the bench. So the question is not whether client work took the week. It is whether there was a bench and what happened to it.

If Ventures and Marketing were both zero and there was idle time, say so plainly. Three months of that means turning work down or hiring, see [[Business Lines#The honest risk in that rule]].
