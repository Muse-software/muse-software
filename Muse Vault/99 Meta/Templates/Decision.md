**Decision.** What was decided, in one or two sentences. Definite, not "we are leaning towards".

**Why.** The reasoning, and what alternative was rejected. The rejected option is the valuable part, because in a year it will look attractive again.

**Consequences.** What this commits us to and what it rules out. Include anything it makes harder.

**Revisit.** A date or a condition, if this should be reconsidered. Leave blank if it is settled.
