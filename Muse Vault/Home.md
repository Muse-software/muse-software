## The founding story

Muse was born out of frustration. The Saudi (and global) AI and software space is full of slop — repetitive content, fake case studies, AI-generated garbage, people claiming things they haven't built. Everyone is shipping something, but almost nobody is shipping something *good*.

The founder has spent years in enterprise — PIF-level organizations, large-scale applications, stakeholder communication at the highest level. That experience is real. It's the reason Muse holds the standard it holds. But it's not the story the site tells first. The story is: we've seen what bad looks like, we know what good looks like, and we're sick of shipping the first one.

Muse is the line. The quality bar. The other choice.

## The three doors (website structure)

The homepage has three doors, not three services. Each door is a capacity Muse offers:

1. **Build** — Production-grade software, shippable AI features, real interfaces. Built fast, stakeholder-friendly, quality UX that feels like a big company built it.
2. **Ventures** — We're building our own products too. The golden egg hunt. Open to collaborating with people who have ideas. Real pipeline, real progress.
3. **Think** — Knowledge sharing, articles, tutorials, showcases, newsletter. Building a name in the Saudi startup/AI/ventures community. Not just sharing knowledge — building influence and brand.

Each door is a card on the homepage with a checkable promise and a CTA. The visitor picks the door that matches them.

## Content strategy

- **Study real companies** — Tenex (global, English, great copy), ZTO, Lean, Salla, Habbar. Take the sections/mechanics that work. Adapt to Muse's voice.
- **No AI-generated copy.** The AI helps with structure and adaptation, not writing. All copy is human-reviewed.
- **English first.** Arabic comes later with a native writer. AI is bad at AR content.
- **Newsletter** — Build from scratch. Subscription-only at start (no payment). Published as articles on the site (SEO) + emailed to subscribers. Phase 2 content, not launch content.
- **Playbooks/archived content** — 28 anonymized articles exist in the archive. They are real work, not AI slop (the dev gave his CV/work to an AI that generated them). But they're not launch content. They're a resource for later framing.
- **Showcases** — Anonymized case studies + live experiments (playable demos, 3D objects, AI agents as experiments on the site). Phase 2.
- **Case studies** — Real work, anonymized. Ready to frame but Phase 2. If prospects want names, they ask; founder's LinkedIn is public.

## The experience signal

Founder's PIF/enterprise background is real and is the context for why Muse holds the standard it does. It lives on the About page, not the homepage. Honest, no name-dropping, no fake logos. "I've been in the room with the big organizations" — the experience speaks through the quality of what you build, not through names. If someone wants to know the names, they can check the founder's LinkedIn.

## Why Muse exists

- Frustration with the amount of slop being shipped in the Saudi and global AI/software space
- Belief that it's easy to do great things — and frustration that almost nobody does
- Desire to build great products, give people amazing experiences, and have real influence
- Client work to build reputation and fund the ventures
- Knowledge sharing and community building — not just tutorials, but becoming a name that the startup/AI/ventures community in Saudi Arabia knows and trusts
- Government and institutional reach — the goal is to be the studio that entities come to, not the one that chases them
