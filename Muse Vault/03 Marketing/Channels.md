## Activation checklist

Do this before publishing anything. An account with no logo and no posts reads as abandoned, which is worse than not existing.

- [ ] Logo applied everywhere, correct variant per shape. Vertical or icon lockup for round avatars, see [[Brand Identity]]
- [ ] Handles identical across platforms, or as close as availability allows. Claim the ones we do not use yet so nobody else does
- [ ] Bio written in Arabic and English, native in both, no banned phrases, see [[Brand Voice]]
- [ ] One clear line saying what we do, understandable by someone who has never heard of us
- [ ] Link to the website, and a plan for what that link points to before launch
- [ ] Cover images and highlight covers where the platform has them
- [ ] Contact route that someone actually monitors. An unanswered DM is worse than no DM channel
- [ ] Location set to Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
- [ ] Access held by more than one person, credentials in a password manager, see [[Tooling Stack]]
- [ ] A pinned or first post that introduces the company. Written last, once the rest is in place

## Priorities

Three active channels done well beat six neglected ones. Recommendation to argue about with the marketing lead:

**Start with two.** One where practitioners are and one visual. Probably X and Instagram, or Instagram and TikTok if the content is going to be video first.

**Add LinkedIn early anyway**, even at low effort, because it is where the client audience is and where an inbound project is most likely to originate. A quiet but complete LinkedIn page still does work when someone checks whether we are real.

**Personal accounts matter more than the company account.** Reach here sits with people, not logos. The founders and team posting under their own names, with the company amplifying, outperforms a brand account posting alone. See [[Influence Strategy#Personal accounts do the heavy lifting]].

## Owned versus rented

Followers on any platform are rented. The algorithm changes and the audience is gone.

- The website is ours
- An email list is ours, and worth starting earlier than feels necessary. The free assets in [[Content Engine]] are the natural reason for someone to give an address
- A WhatsApp channel or community is worth considering, since WhatsApp is where people here actually are, see [[Localization Playbook#Channels]]
