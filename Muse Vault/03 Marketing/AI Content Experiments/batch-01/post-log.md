# Post log, Batch 01

This is the measurement model from Plan.md section 4A made concrete. One row per published post. Fill in as each experiment actually goes out. Do not backfill from memory; if a field is unknown, leave it blank rather than guess.

## Definitions

**Substantive reply.** A comment or reply that adds an opinion, a counter-point, a lived experience, or a specific question. More than one clause. Not an emoji, not "great post," not a tag with no words. Spam and bots excluded.

**Real conversation.** A back and forth of at least three messages with a real person, in comments or DMs, or any exchange that surfaces a need, a referral, or a follow-up we act on. This is the number that connects to revenue.

**Primary-action rate.** Primary metric count divided by accounts reached, per 1,000 reached. Compare rates across posts, not raw totals, since raw counts favour whichever post got amplified.

## Fair comparison note

Six posts show direction, not proof. Rank by primary-action rate, watch for confounds (which account posted, day and time, outside amplification), and treat one strong post as a hypothesis to re-run, never as a conclusion to scale on immediately.

## Secondary diagnostics, tracked but never celebrated alone

Followers, likes, impressions, and reach move for reasons unrelated to the work. Record them per post if useful for spotting confounds, never as the headline result.

## Choosing the next batch

- Rank by primary-action rate, not raw reach.
- Keep topic and format pairs that earned saves, replies, or conversations. Retire the ones that earned only likes and impressions.
- Read every substantive reply and DM. Recurring questions become the next content calendar, written by the audience.
- Re-run a winner in a second variant, same topic in a different format or channel, before scaling it. One strong post is a hypothesis, not proof.
- Watch confounds: reach gaps, which account posted, day and time, and any outside amplification.
