# X thread, Experiment 1. Model picking

1/ Three major AI releases landed in one month. Each gives you a different reason to switch.

2/ Claude Opus 5 [1], OpenAI's GPT-5.6 family, Sol, Terra, Luna [2], and Google's Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and 3.5 Flash Cyber [3], all landed within a few weeks of each other.

3/ Anthropic calls Opus 5 its new default on Claude Max [1]. OpenAI says Sol sets a new state of the art on its own benchmark [2]. Google is explicit that its Flash trio is not even the flagship line, that is still Gemini Pro [3].

4/ Three companies, three different sales pitches: a new default, a benchmark lead, and lower-token Flash models. None of those claims measures your work.

5/ So pick for your work, not the leaderboard: your task, your language including Arabic, your latency and cost, tested on your own examples.
