# Muse AI content experiments, Batch 01

## Task

Create a research and execution plan for a first batch of 6 social content experiments for Muse Studios. The final deliverable will be a Muse-branded PDF plus editable source files. Each experiment must be ready to publish as either a 4-5 slide carousel, an X thread, or both.

The user wants content that has a strong chance of earning comments, saves, shares, quote posts, and real conversations. The content does not need a never-seen-before premise. It can adapt a proven format or discussion, but it must use Muse's own framing and branding. Every claim must come from real current sources.

Current date: 2026-08-05, Riyadh time.

The vault contains a deliberate tension. The user wants engagement-optimised posts, but Muse rejects dishonest engagement bait, comment pods, fake conflict, reposting other people's work as its own, and empty engagement farming. Resolve this by designing honest, source-backed posts that invite a real opinion or useful disagreement. Do not weaken the user's goal of reach and engagement.

## Audience and channel assumptions

- Start with practitioners and peers: designers, developers, product people, AI builders, students, and other studios in Saudi Arabia and the GCC.
- Buyers and institutional readers are a useful secondary audience.
- X and Instagram are the main experiment channels. LinkedIn receives adapted versions.
- Personal founder and team accounts do the heavy lifting. Muse amplifies and keeps the official archive.
- English first. Arabic is a later native-writing pass, not a machine translation.

## Required content mix

Plan exactly 6 experiments with variety across:

- Current AI news or a consequential model or platform release
- A genuinely useful new AI tool or workflow
- A recent credible AI research finding
- AI plus behavioural economics or human decision-making
- A debatable opinion grounded in evidence
- A Saudi or Arabic product-quality angle, ideally Arabic AI, RTL, local context, or regional adoption

The six do not need to map one-to-one if a stronger mix emerges. At least 4 should be tied to material published in the last 90 days. One or two can use older landmark research if it makes the topic stronger.

## Research requirements

Use WebSearch and WebFetch for current reconnaissance. Prefer first-party sources, original papers, official product announcements, model cards, reputable research labs, and credible journalism for context. Avoid SEO roundups and unattributed social posts.

For every candidate, capture:

- Exact source URL
- Source title
- Publisher or author
- Publication date
- Verbatim evidence excerpt that supports the central claim
- What is genuinely new or timely
- What is interpretation rather than source fact
- One independent corroborating source for consequential or disputed claims

Do not include unverified 2026 products, releases, numbers, quotes, or dates. If a page cannot be fetched or the claim cannot be checked, reject the candidate.

## Format design requirements

For each experiment, the final content must include:

- A one-line premise
- Best format: Instagram carousel, X carousel, X thread, single post, or hybrid
- A complete 4-5 slide structure with draft on-slide copy, not just slide topics
- A strong but truthful hook
- A specific question that invites genuine disagreement or experience
- A short caption or thread opener
- Why it can earn engagement, separated into likely comments, saves, shares, and quote posts
- Risks, including oversimplification, stale news, weak evidence, outrage without substance, or brand mismatch
- Distribution move: who posts first, how Muse amplifies, and how LinkedIn is adapted
- Founder interaction playbook for the first day, including replies before posting, replies after posting, quote-post targets, and useful follow-ups
- Success hypothesis and the one primary metric

## Measurement requirements

The final batch should be run as a controlled learning loop rather than six random posts.

- Define one primary metric per post based on its job: saves, shares, substantive replies, profile visits, or real conversations.
- Track secondary diagnostics but do not celebrate followers, likes, impressions, or reach as success on their own.
- Include a simple normalised comparison so posts of different reach can be compared fairly.
- Define what counts as a substantive reply or real conversation.
- Include a reusable post log with hypothesis, format, topic, source age, channel, account, primary metric, result, quality notes, and next iteration.
- Recommend how to choose the next batch from the first batch's results without pretending 6 posts are statistically conclusive.

## Voice and document rules

- Plain, specific, human, warm, not corporate.
- No AI marketing language.
- No invented numbers or fake certainty.
- No em dash, en dash, guillemets, or curly quotes.
- No banned Muse phrases: innovative solutions, cutting edge technology, disrupting the industry, seamless, synergy, leveraging, empowering, unlocking, passionate about, best in class, next level, thrilled to announce, excited to share.
- Never attack a named Saudi company for reach.
- It is acceptable to create respectful tension around a claim, trade-off, or practice.
