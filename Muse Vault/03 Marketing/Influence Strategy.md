# Influence Strategy

What the audience is actually for. Reach with no purpose behind it is a hobby, so this note names the four things we intend to convert it into.

## 1. Inbound client work

The most immediate return. What it looks like when it is working:

- Someone remembers us unprompted when their company needs product, design or brand work
- A friend mentions they need this and our name comes up in the conversation
- Someone inside a company tells their vendor management to look at us
- A prospect arrives already knowing the standard of our work, so the conversation starts at scope rather than at credentials

That last one is the real prize. Inbound clients arrive pre sold, which means better projects, less negotiation and higher prices, see [[Scoping & Pricing]].

**What produces it.** Craft content that demonstrates competence, work shown in public, and being on LinkedIn where the buyers are, see [[Channels]]. Case studies once client work exists, see [[Studio#Case studies]].

## 2. Distribution for our own products

Every venture launch otherwise starts from zero. Publishing an MVP to nobody is the most expensive part of the model in [[Product Philosophy]], because the attempt can fail from invisibility rather than from being wrong.

An audience does not replace paid acquisition for a consumer app, and it would be a mistake to pretend otherwise, see [[Audience Research#Three audiences, one account]]. What it does give us is a first hundred users who are willing to try things and tell us what is broken, which is worth more at MVP stage than a thousand indifferent installs.

## 3. Institutional access

Government entities that support startups, ministries, accelerators, funds, large organisations. The goal is that our name is already familiar when a conversation starts.

Being honest about what actually earns attention at that level, because follower count is not it:

- **Shipped work that exists and is used.** One live product beats any amount of positioning
- **Being the recognised name in something specific.** "The people who do Arabic product quality properly" is a position. "A software studio" is not
- **Showing up in person.** Programmes, events, demo days, panels. Relationships here are built in rooms, see [[Community & Events#Speaking at other people's events]]
- **Saudi credentials.** A Saudi team building Saudi products for Saudi users aligns with what these entities exist to support, see [[Positioning#Saudi first]]
- **Being easy to say yes to.** When someone inside an entity wants to champion us, they need something to point at. A real website, a real product, a clean deck

Worth mapping the entities and programmes deliberately rather than hoping to bump into them. Not done yet.

## 4. Standing for the people here

Explicit part of the deal, see [[Positioning#Long term goal]]. Anyone who works at Muse should become more known and more employable by working here.

This is not charity. It is why good people join a small unknown company instead of a large known one, and it is a real retention argument that costs nothing. It also compounds back into the company, because a team of people with reputations produces far more reach than a logo does.

Practical: people post under their own names, get credited by name in our work, and are put forward to speak rather than the company being put forward.

## Personal accounts do the heavy lifting

Worth stating plainly because it changes how effort is allocated. In this market, personal accounts outperform company accounts by a wide margin. People follow people. A logo posting a tutorial gets a fraction of the reach the same tutorial gets from a named person.

The model that works:

- The team posts as themselves, in their own voice, about their own work
- The company account amplifies, collects and holds the official material
- Nobody is required to post. Anyone who wants to is supported with time, editing help and ideas

The risk to accept knowingly: reach built on a person leaves when the person leaves. That is a fair trade for reach that actually exists, and the mitigation is more than one person doing it plus the owned surfaces in [[Channels#Owned versus rented]].

## Batch content system

The `AI Content Experiments` system (`03 Marketing/AI Content Experiments/`) produces source-backed social batches designed for the personal-account model. Each experiment includes a founder interaction plan for the first 24 hours: useful replies before posting, substantive engagement after posting, and quote-post targets that extend the conversation. This operationalises the personal-account-first approach at batch level.

## What we will not trade the audience for

Trust is the asset. Everything on this page depends on it, and it is easier to keep than to rebuild.

- No promoting things we would not use
- No undisclosed paid promotion
- No inflated numbers or invented results
- No engagement farming
- No attacking named local companies for reach
