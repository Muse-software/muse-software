## How to do a teardown properly

The point is not a feature list. Anyone can list features. The point is to work out **why it works**, which is usually one or two decisions rather than the whole product.

Method:

1. Use it seriously for a week. Actually use it, as the intended user, not a five minute click through
2. Notice where it is fast and where it is slow, and ask whether that was a choice
3. Find what it refuses to do. What a mature product leaves out is the strongest signal of what its makers learned
4. Read its bad reviews. The one star reviews on a good product tell you where the real gaps are
5. Then ask what changes for a Saudi user, against [[Localization Playbook]]
