# Saudi Market Notes

Working context. Everything here is a hypothesis until it has a source next to it. Do not put an unverified number in a client proposal.

## Why this market is interesting for a product company

- Young population with high smartphone penetration and high digital service adoption
- Government led digital transformation has made people comfortable with digital services, so the education problem is smaller than it would be elsewhere
- Consumer spending on digital services is strong
- Many categories are still served by international products that were never adapted for here, which is the entire thesis in [[Product Philosophy]]
- Small businesses are under digitised, which is the thesis behind [[Productised Solutions]]

## Structural things worth knowing

- iOS share is high relative to global averages, and skews toward the audiences that pay for software. Affects build order. Verify current figures
- The domestic payment infrastructure is mature: Mada, Apple Pay, wallets, BNPL. Payments are not a barrier
- WhatsApp is the default communication layer for consumers and businesses alike
- English is widely used in business, but consumer products live in Arabic
- Riyadh, Jeddah and the Eastern Province are the main commercial concentrations, and Riyadh is growing fast

## What this means for us

**The gap is adaptation, not invention.** Good products exist globally. Very few have been properly adapted for here. That is a durable advantage because it requires local knowledge rather than capital.

**Quality is a differentiator.** Much of the local software market is built to a price. A studio holding an actual [[Quality Bar]] stands out immediately. This is what makes [[Positioning]] viable.

**Small business software is wide open.** Most cafes, salons and clinics run on WhatsApp and paper. The barrier is distribution and support, not product, see [[Productised Solutions]].
