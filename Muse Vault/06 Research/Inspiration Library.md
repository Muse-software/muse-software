# Inspiration Library

Products worth studying. A holding list, not a build list. When one becomes serious, it gets a full [[Teardown]] and a venture note.

This note is more important than it looks. The venture model in [[Product Philosophy]] works by taking enough cheap shots that one lands, which means the candidate list is the real input to the whole pillar. Right now it is three products from one conversation, all in one domain, which is not enough to choose from. Widening it is the first job in [[Ventures]], see [[Open Questions#4. Which category we start in]].

The filter for adding something: a proven product with real users somewhere else, and no properly localised version here. Category does not matter.

## Worth adding

Categories where the adapt and localise thesis probably holds. Unresearched.

- Habit tracking and streak products
- Personal finance and budgeting, given the local payment infrastructure
- Local services marketplaces
- Practice management for small clinics
- Field service and dispatch for trades
- Community and membership tools
- Anything where the leading product has no Arabic version

## How to use this note

Add products as you notice them, with one line on why. Do not write a teardown until it is a candidate. The purpose of this list is to be scanned once a month for patterns, not to be complete.

Also worth recording the opposite: products that tried in this market and failed. Failure tells you more about local constraints than success does. That list is currently empty and worth filling.
