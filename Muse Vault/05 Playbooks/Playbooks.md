# Playbooks

How we actually work. These apply to [[Ventures]] and [[Studio]] equally. Client work does not get a lower standard.

| Playbook | Use it when |
|---|---|
| [[Build Loop]] | Any piece of work, from idea to shipped |
| [[Localization Playbook]] | Every project. This is the one that matters most here |
| [[Quality Bar]] | Before calling anything done |
| [[Launch Checklist]] | Before anything goes in front of real users |
| [[Measurement]] | Setting up a product so you can tell if it works |
| [[AI Guidelines]] | Anyone suggests adding AI to something |

## Missing, worth writing

- [ ] **Adaptation policy.** What we take from reference products and what we never take. Blocked on [[Open Questions#2. How close is too close]]. This is the most important gap
- [ ] **Design review.** Who reviews what, at which point, and what they check
- [ ] **Handover.** What a client receives at the end of an engagement
- [ ] **Support.** Needed before any of our own products has a user who depends on it
- [ ] **Incident response.** What happens when a live product breaks at 11pm

## How playbooks stay alive

A playbook that is never edited is a playbook nobody follows. Every retrospective should change at least one of these files. If a project went badly in a way that was avoidable, the fix belongs here rather than in someone's memory.
