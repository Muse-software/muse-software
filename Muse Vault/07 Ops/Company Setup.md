# Company Setup

A list of things to confirm, not advice. Requirements change and the details matter, so verify each item with the relevant authority or a qualified advisor before acting. Record what you find, with a date, so the next person does not have to look it up again.

## Entity

- [ ] Legal structure decided. Sole establishment against limited liability company, and what that means for liability and future investment
- [ ] Commercial registration in place, with the activity codes covering software development, design services and any product sales
- [ ] Confirm the activity codes actually cover selling subscription software, not just development services. Easy to get wrong and annoying to fix
- [ ] Municipality licence if premises are required
- [ ] Chamber of commerce membership
- [ ] National address registered for the business

## Tax and financial

- [ ] VAT registration status confirmed against the current threshold
- [ ] E-invoicing requirements understood and the system in place before invoicing starts
- [ ] Zakat and income tax obligations understood, and filing dates in a calendar
- [ ] Corporate bank account opened
- [ ] Accounting handled by a real accountant from month one, not a spreadsheet you intend to tidy later
- [ ] Payment gateway or merchant account, before any product collects money

## Employment

- [ ] Labour registration in place before the first hire
- [ ] Social insurance registration
- [ ] Saudisation requirements understood for the size and category of the company
- [ ] Employment contract template reviewed by someone qualified
- [ ] Understand what applies to contractors and freelancers as opposed to employees

## Intellectual property

- [ ] Trademark search and filing for the name and mark, see [[Open Questions#1. The name]]
- [ ] Domain names secured, including the obvious defensive variants
- [ ] Position decided on IP ownership in client contracts, see [[Engagement Lifecycle#3. Agreement]]
- [ ] Confirm that work produced by contractors is assigned to the company in writing

## Data protection

This one is not optional and it applies from the first user, not from the first thousand.

- [ ] Personal data protection law obligations understood, including the implementing regulations
- [ ] Whether data must be stored inside the Kingdom, and under what conditions transfer out is allowed
- [ ] What lawful basis and consent look like for each product
- [ ] Privacy policy in Arabic and English that describes what the products actually do
- [ ] A named person responsible for data protection
- [ ] Any registration or notification requirement checked
- [ ] Health data obligations checked before any health or clinical product, including a fitness or nutrition app that stores anything medical

## Contracts to have ready

- [ ] Client services agreement
- [ ] Statement of work or scope annexe template
- [ ] Non disclosure agreement, both directions
- [ ] Contractor agreement with IP assignment
- [ ] Subscription terms of service, before any product charges monthly

## Insurance

- [ ] Medical insurance for employees, which is generally mandatory
- [ ] Consider professional indemnity, since building software that handles other people's money or data carries real exposure

## Record decisions

Every answer found goes in [[Decision Log]] with the date and the source. Compliance questions get asked repeatedly over years, and a dated note with a source saves the same research three times.
