# Ops

The unglamorous layer. Company setup, tools, money, hiring.

- [[Company Setup]] entity, registrations, compliance

- [[Money]] pricing, costs, runway
- [[Hiring]] roles, when, how

## Principle

Ops decisions are cheap to make early and expensive to make late. Choosing an accounting setup in year one takes an afternoon. Fixing three years of bad records takes a month and a specialist.

The same applies to anything with a compliance dimension: data protection, invoicing, contracts. Set it up correctly once, then stop thinking about it.
