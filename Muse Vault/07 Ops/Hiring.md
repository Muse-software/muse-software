# Hiring

## Principle

Hire when the constraint is capacity, not when it is capability you have not tried to build. A small team that ships beats a larger team that coordinates.

[[Positioning]] says premium and craftsmanship. That is a hiring standard before it is a marketing line. One person who holds [[Quality Bar]] is worth more than three who need it enforced on them.

## Likely order

Argue with this, do not follow it blindly.

1. **Marketing.** Happening now, see [[Marketing Lead Brief]]. A whole pillar with nobody on it, which is why nothing has been published
2. **A designer who can build, or an engineer who can design.** The most valuable profile for a small product studio. Removes the handoff that slows everything down
3. **Someone who writes Arabic properly.** Not a translator. Someone who can write product and marketing copy natively. [[Localization Playbook]] is unenforceable without this, and it might be a contractor rather than a hire. The marketing lead may cover part of it
4. **Engineering depth,** once client work exceeds what Abdullah can deliver
5. **A second person whose main focus is Ventures,** once client revenue supports it. This is what protects the long game when the bench disappears, see [[Money#Funding the other two pillars]]
6. **Someone who sells,** once inbound alone is not filling the pipeline. The hire most technical founders delay too long

## What to look for

- Has shipped something real that a stranger has used. A portfolio of concepts is a warning sign
- Cares about the details others skip: empty states, error messages, the second language
- Can explain a decision they made and what they gave up for it
- Works in Arabic and English comfortably
- Comfortable being visible. Not required, but this company builds in public, see [[Content Engine]]

## What to screen out

- Impressive vocabulary, thin work
- Cannot name anything they got wrong
- Needs a fully specified task to start
- Treats Arabic support as someone else's job

## Practical

- [ ] Employment and social insurance registration in place before the first hire, see [[Company Setup]]
- [ ] Saudisation requirements understood for the company size
- [ ] Contract template reviewed by someone qualified
- [ ] Decide the position on remote and hybrid before advertising, since it changes the candidate pool
- [ ] Salary bands, so offers are not improvised
- [ ] Decide whether any equity or profit share is on the table, and how that would work with the entity structure

## First week

Whatever the role, the first week should end with something shipped, however small. It teaches the standard faster than any document, and it tells you quickly whether the hire was right.

Point them at [[Vault Guide]], [[Quality Bar]], [[Localization Playbook]] and [[Brand Voice]] on day one. Those four notes are the culture in written form.
