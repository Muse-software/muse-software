# Scoping & Pricing

Nothing here is decided yet. It is the framework and the questions that need answers before the first proposal goes out.

## Principles

**Price the outcome, not the time.** Day rates make us a commodity and cap our income at hours available. They also punish us for getting faster, which is the wrong incentive.

**Fixed price needs fixed scope.** Fixed price on vague scope is how studios lose money. If they will not define scope, sell Discovery first as its own paid engagement.

**Never the cheapest.** [[Positioning]] says premium. Competing on price contradicts everything else and attracts clients who will treat us as interchangeable. Losing on price is a qualification signal, not a loss.

**Deposit before work.** Typically a third up front, with the rest tied to milestones.

## Sizing method

For any piece of work, estimate the three separately:

1. **Delivery**, the actual design and build
2. **Overhead**, meetings, revisions, coordination, the client's internal delays. Realistically a large share, and it is the part that gets forgotten
3. **Risk**, unknowns, integrations with systems we have not seen, decision makers we have not met

Then check the total against what the outcome is worth to the client. If the thing we build saves them a salary, that is the frame, not our hours.

## Signals to raise the price

- Multiple stakeholders, especially across departments
- Integration with a system we have not seen
- Deadline driven by something external, an event, a campaign, a regulatory date
- They have already failed at this once with someone else
- They want us to work inside their process and tools

## Signals to decline

Turning down bad work is a pricing decision. Write the list before you are desperate enough to ignore it.

- Budget under whatever the minimum turns out to be, because small projects carry the same overhead as large ones
- No access to users
- Payment terms we cannot live with, or a client with a reputation for slow payment
- Scope that will not be defined and no willingness to pay for Discovery
- Work on the list in [[Service Catalogue#What we do not sell]]
