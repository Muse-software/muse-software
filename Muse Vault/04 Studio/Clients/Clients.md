# Clients

One note per client, created from [[Client]] at the first conversation, kept alive for the whole relationship.

No clients yet.

## Why one note per client and not per project

A client is a relationship, not a transaction. Second and third projects come from the same relationship, and the context that makes them easy sits in one place: who decides, what they care about, what went wrong last time, what they said no to and why.

Each project inside the client note gets its own section rather than its own note. Split into separate notes only when a client has more than three projects.

## Conventions

- Title is the company name as they write it, not a code
- Status in frontmatter, one of: lead, proposal, active, paused, done, lost
- Decisions get a date and stay in the note forever, even when they are reversed
- Money is recorded in the note, not just in the accounting tool
- When an engagement ends, keep the note and mark it done. Never delete a client note
