## Brand and marketing

| Service | What the client gets |
|---|---|

| Brand refresh | An existing identity tightened rather than replaced |
| Graphic design | Social assets, decks, print, campaign material |

| Content and social | Content plan, production, and running the accounts |

Two things worth holding on to when selling this:

**We are strongest where brand meets product.** An identity that survives contact with a real interface, Arabic included. Most branding studios hand over a logo and a PDF and have never had to build with it.

**Our own accounts are the portfolio.** Which is a reason to get [[Channels#Activation checklist]] done properly. It is hard to sell social work from empty accounts.

## Packaging

A menu invites line item shopping and price comparison. Named engagements do not. Worth fixing these as products with a stated shape:

- **Product Audit**, fixed scope, fixed price, two to three weeks. Best possible first engagement. Low commitment for them, and it usually reveals the real work
- **Discovery Sprint**, problem to defined scope, fixed price
- **Build**, scoped from a completed Discovery
- **Brand Sprint**, identity or refresh, fixed scope and price
- **Product Partner**, monthly retainer, ongoing product and design capacity

The Audit is worth building first. It is the easiest thing to say yes to, and it puts us in the diagnosing position rather than the quoting position.

## What we do not sell

Hold this list, because these requests will come and they are how a product company turns into a body shop:

- Body shopping. Developers rented by the month with no product involvement
- Anything where we have no access to users and no say in the decisions
- Work at a price that requires cutting the quality bar to make money on it, see [[Quality Bar]]

Note what is not on this list any more. Brochure websites and standalone brand work are acceptable when the work is good and priced properly. Early on, a well paid identity project that funds two weeks of venture work is a good trade. The thing to avoid is not the category, it is the client relationship where we are treated as interchangeable, see [[Scoping & Pricing#Signals to decline]].
