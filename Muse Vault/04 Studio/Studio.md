## How the work is meant to arrive

Inbound, eventually. Somebody who has been following our content, or whose friend mentioned us, or who was told about us by a colleague. That is the whole point of [[Marketing]], and it is what makes the difference between choosing projects and taking whatever appears, see [[Influence Strategy#1. Inbound client work]].

Until that engine is running, work comes from the network and from people Abdullah already knows. Worth being honest that this is the position for now.

## Staying a product company while doing client work

Client work is the fastest route to becoming the agency [[Positioning]] says we are not. Two habits protect against it.

**Sell outcomes, not hours.** The moment we quote a day rate against a list of screens, we are a vendor and the relationship is transactional forever.

**Keep the product view.** A client asking for a redesign usually has a business problem underneath. Finding it and naming it is what makes us a partner, and it is what makes the second project happen without a pitch.

Neither habit requires refusing brand or marketing projects. That work is on the menu, see [[Service Catalogue]]. It requires doing it as people who think about outcomes rather than deliverables.
