# Roles

Who owns what, by role. One person responsible per pillar, everyone supporting across all three, see [[Business Lines#Who owns what]]. Unowned work is the work that does not happen.

This vault describes roles rather than individuals, see [[People#Roles, not names]].

## Still unassigned, in order of what it costs to leave empty

**Quality.** [[Quality Bar]] is the claim in [[Positioning]] made real. If nobody owns it, it degrades under deadline pressure every time, and nobody notices until a client does. Natural fit for the technical lead, worth confirming.

**Arabic and localisation.** [[Localization Playbook]] is the competitive advantage, and it cannot be owned by someone who does not write Arabic well. Might be a hire or a trusted contractor. The marketing lead may cover part of it if they write Arabic natively.

**Tools and access.** Nobody owns the password manager, backups or who has access to what. Cheap to fix now, expensive after the first incident. See [[Tooling Stack]].

## Ownership means

- You decide, and you can be overruled only by an explicit decision recorded in [[Decision Log]]
- You keep the relevant notes current
- When it goes wrong, it is your problem to fix, not to explain
- You ask for support early. Everyone supports every pillar, and an owner who never asks becomes a bottleneck rather than a hero

## Worth agreeing with each owner

The same short list applies to every role, and it is better settled at the start than after something works.

- [ ] Arrangement: employment, contract, part time, equity, or a share of what a venture makes
- [ ] Time commitment, honestly stated, and what it looks like in a normal week
- [ ] How their main focus is protected when client work is busy
- [ ] Who decides when they and the founder disagree
- [ ] Whether they want to be visible in content. Not everyone does, and it should be a choice, see [[Content Engine]]
- [ ] Access: accounts, password manager, this vault, see [[Tooling Stack]]
