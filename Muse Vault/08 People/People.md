# People

How the team is structured, and the network worth building. Ownership detail is in [[Roles]].

## Roles, not names

This vault describes the team by role rather than by individual. Notes stay accurate when people change, nothing reads as a personal file, and anything here can be shared or handed to someone new without editing it first.

Same rule in anything we produce from it, including [[Company Explainer]] and anything client facing.

Person notes are for the external network below, not for the team.

## The team

| Role | Main focus |
|---|---|
| Founder | Company direction, client relationships, money. Owns [[Studio]] |
| Technical lead | Development and architecture. The main technical person, and the technical side of every engagement |
| Design lead | Design and product direction on our own apps. Owns [[Ventures]] |
| Marketing lead | Audience, content, the public voice. Owns [[Marketing]]. In conversation, see [[Marketing Lead Brief]] |
| Client delivery support | Works alongside the founder on engagements |

One person is responsible per pillar and everyone supports across all three, see [[Business Lines#Who owns what]]. Main focus is what someone is accountable for, not the only thing they touch.

Roles not yet filled, and what it costs to leave them empty, are in [[Roles#Still unassigned, in order of what it costs to leave empty]]. Hiring order in [[Hiring]].

## Network

Empty. Worth building deliberately rather than hoping.

Categories worth having someone in:

- A lawyer who understands technology contracts and data protection
- An accountant who handles e-invoicing and VAT properly
- An Arabic writer or editor, see [[Hiring]]
- Designers and engineers worth hiring later, tracked before you need them
- People who run local tech and startup events, since speaking at their events is the cheapest route to reach and to institutional relationships, see [[Community & Events#Speaking at other people's events]]
- Named humans inside the entities that back startups here, see [[Influence Strategy#3. Institutional access]]
- Other Saudi studio founders. Competitors, but also the people who will tell you what a client is actually like before you sign
- Operators inside whatever category a venture ends up targeting. The local integrations in [[Product Philosophy#Localisation is the product, not a translation layer]] are relationships before they are code, so these contacts are worth having before the product needs them

## Why keep person notes for the network

Small business runs on relationships and memory. A note recording what someone cares about, what you discussed and what you promised is the difference between a network and a contact list.

Practical rule: after any meeting that matters, spend two minutes on the person note, using [[Person]]. What they said, what you agreed, when to follow up. That two minutes is what makes the next conversation feel effortless.
