# Journal

Daily notes, weekly reviews and the decision log. This is the thinking layer. The rest of the vault holds conclusions, this folder holds how you got there.

## Daily notes

One per day, `YYYY-MM-DD`, created with the Daily Note command. Configured to use [[Daily Note]] automatically.

Do not force it. A daily note is worth writing on a day when something happened: a decision, a conversation, a realisation, a thing that broke. Skipping quiet days is fine and better than filling them with nothing.

## Weekly review

Once a week, twenty minutes, using [[Weekly Review]]. The single habit that keeps this vault from becoming a folder of abandoned notes.

Three jobs:

1. Empty [[Inbox]]
2. Look at the one metric per live product, see [[Measurement]]
3. Decide what next week is actually for

## Decision log

[[Decision Log]] holds anything structural. Separate from daily notes because decisions need to be findable years later, and nobody scrolls back through daily notes to find one.

## Why bother

Two reasons that hold up.

**You will forget why.** In eight months you will look at a choice and not remember the constraint that drove it. Written reasoning stops you relitigating something you already settled.

**It is the raw material for everything else.** Case studies, the website, investor conversations, hiring. All of it is easier to write from a record than from memory.
