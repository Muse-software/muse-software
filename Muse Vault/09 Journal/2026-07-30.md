## Afternoon: Abdullah corrected the strategy

The vault was built from a ChatGPT session that had not understood him properly. He restated the strategy directly, and it is now the source of truth. Everything below is settled and recorded in [[Decision Log]].

**The three pillars changed.** Not Ventures, Studio, Local Solutions. It is [[Ventures]], [[Marketing]] and [[Studio]] client services. Marketing was never a pillar in the old material, which is exactly why nothing has been published. Productised local business software is parked in [[Productised Solutions]] and the `04 Solutions` folder is gone.

**Client services are the main income at the start,** stated plainly, with no discomfort about it. The old material was slightly embarrassed about client work and kept insisting we are not an agency. The real position is that services fund the other two pillars and that is the plan, not a compromise.

**The capacity rule got answered.** One owner per pillar, everyone supporting across all three, client work first on shared capacity, and owners keeping their main focus through a busy period. The old note wanted a fixed non negotiable percentage. This is more honest and more likely to survive a real deadline. See [[Business Lines#Who owns what]].

**The fitness examples are examples.** Abdullah used a localised fitness tracker to explain what deep localisation means, gym integrations and all. It was written up on the first pass as the intended first build, which was wrong and is now corrected. Fitness is a real candidate, the category is open, and the candidate list needs widening before anything is chosen. See [[Open Questions#4. Which category we start in]].

**Ventures are a portfolio, not a research programme.** The old [[Venture Pipeline]] had a validation stage demanding ten interviews before design. He does not want that. The proof is that the product already works elsewhere. Build the localised MVP, publish, and let a traction gate decide. Rewritten accordingly.

**The identity is settled.** Orange on maroon, v0.02. The dark glassmorphism and metallics direction is dropped. Inferred from the brand being finished and already going onto the accounts rather than stated outright, so worth confirming.

**The voice is not corporate.** This was the sharpest contradiction with the old material, which said calm, refined, executive. He wants content close to people: working clips, the team as themselves, fun. [[Brand Voice]] now carries two registers.

**We do sell brand, marketing and graphics work.** The old [[Service Catalogue]] explicitly refused standalone brand work. Reversed.

## Worth noticing

The most useful thing to come out of writing the marketing notes is that the audience is three audiences, and one account cannot serve all of them well. Practitioners, buyers, consumers. Not decided, see [[Open Questions#5. Which audience we build first]], with a recommendation in [[Audience Research]] worth arguing about.

The adaptation policy is now the biggest gap in the vault. Under the old plan there was a long research phase between the reference product and our version. Under this one there is not, which makes the written line between adapting and copying more important, not less. See [[Open Questions#2. How close is too close]].
