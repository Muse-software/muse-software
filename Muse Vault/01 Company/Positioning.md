# Positioning

## One line

A Saudi product company that builds its own software and helps other businesses build theirs properly.

## What we are

A product company that funds itself with client work. Premium in craft, human in how it talks. Business first, at startup speed.

Six things the brand should communicate:

- Craftsmanship
- Innovation
- Quality
- Long term thinking
- Execution
- Trust

## Saudi first

Not decoration. It shows up as specific commitments:

- Arabic and RTL are first class in everything we build, see [[Localization Playbook]]
- We hire Saudis and give people here work that builds their careers
- We would rather pass a job to another good Saudi studio than watch it go outside the Kingdom
- Our products are built for how people here actually live, which is the only durable advantage we have over a foreign product with a bigger team

## What we are not

The market will try to put us in one of these boxes:

- Not a low cost development shop
- Not a body shop renting out developers by the month
- Not a freelancer collective

Note what is missing from that list. We do take client projects, including branding and marketing work, and that is most of the income at the start. The distinction is trajectory, not denial. An agency's ceiling is the hours it can sell. Ours is not, because [[Ventures]] and [[Marketing]] compound while client work pays the bills.

The tell is how a conversation starts. A prospect who opens with a page count and a deadline is shopping for a vendor. One who opens with a problem and a user is shopping for a partner. Both can be good business. They get scoped and priced differently, see [[Scoping & Pricing]].

## How we prove it rather than claim it

Innovation is demonstrated through process, not adjectives. Banned phrases are in [[Brand Voice]].

- We ship, then iterate, and the iteration is visible to users
- We use AI where it changes the outcome, not where it decorates a pitch, see [[AI Guidelines]]
- We localise deeply rather than translating, see [[Localization Playbook]]
- We hold a written quality bar, see [[Quality Bar]]
- We work in public, so the standard is visible before anyone has to take our word for it, see [[Content Engine]]

## Long term goal

A respected Saudi product company known for shipping exceptional software. Recognised for creating successful digital products, helping businesses modernise, launching valuable software, and raising the general quality of software experiences in the Kingdom.

Two things that goal is meant to buy, and the reason [[Marketing]] is a pillar rather than a task:

**Institutional access.** Being known well enough that the entities backing startups here, ministries and large organisations already know the name when a conversation starts. See [[Influence Strategy]].

**Career standing for the people here.** Anyone who works at Muse should leave more known and more employable than they arrived, whether they stay two years or ten. That is part of the deal, and it is why the team posts under their own names rather than only behind a logo.
