# Deck Copy v0.03

Replacement for the About, Mission, Vision and Values pages in [[Brand Guideline PDF]] v0.02. The old copy describes a custom website development agency, uses most of the phrases banned in [[Brand Voice]], and was written by a model rather than decided by anyone. It is out.

This note is the copy and paste source for the deck, the website and the social bios. Written from [[Positioning]] and [[Business Lines]].

Arabic below is a native draft, not a translation of the English. It still needs a read by someone who writes Arabic well before it goes anywhere public, see [[Brand Voice#Arabic voice]].

## About

**Short, one line.** For bios, the website header, anywhere with no room.

> A Saudi product company. We build our own software, and we help other businesses build theirs properly.

**Medium, for a deck page or an about section.**

> Muse is a product company in Riyadh. We build our own apps, and we work with businesses that want their product built properly.
>
> Our own products start from something that already works somewhere in the world. We build the version that fits here, publish it, and keep going with whatever people actually use. Arabic first, real data, and the details most products skip.
>
> Alongside that we work with companies on product, design, engineering and brand. Same standard either way.

**Long, for the website about page.**

> Muse is a product company based in Riyadh.
>
> We do two things. We build and publish our own apps, and we work with businesses on theirs.
>
> Our own products do not start from a blank page. Plenty of good software already exists and already works, so we start there, and we build the version that belongs here. That means Arabic as the first language rather than a translation layer, and it means going deep enough into how people here actually live that a foreign product with a bigger team could not follow us.
>
> With clients we work on product, design, engineering, brand and marketing. Some engagements are a few weeks, some run for a year. What does not change is the standard: real data, every state designed, nothing shipped that we would not use ourselves.
>
> We are Saudi, we hire Saudis, and we would rather see good work stay in the Kingdom.

## Mission

> Build software that people here actually want to use, and raise the standard of what gets shipped in the Kingdom.

## Vision

> A Saudi product company known for shipping exceptional software, whose products are used every day and whose work sets the bar locally.

Deliberately avoided: leading, premier, pioneering, world class. See the banned list in [[Brand Voice]].

## Values

The list in v0.02 is fine. The padding around it is not, so here it is with one plain line each.

| Value | What it means here |
|---|---|
| Craftsmanship | The details nobody is paid to notice get done anyway |
| Innovation | Shown in what we ship, never claimed in a sentence |
| Quality | There is a written bar and nothing goes out below it, see [[Quality Bar]] |
| Long term thinking | We choose the option that is better in two years |
| Execution | Shipped beats planned |
| Trust | We say what is true, including when it costs us the work |

## Arabic, longer

**About, medium**

> ميوز شركة منتجات رقمية في الرياض. نبني تطبيقاتنا الخاصة، ونعمل مع الشركات التي تريد بناء منتجها بشكل صحيح.
>
> منتجاتنا تبدأ من فكرة أثبتت نجاحها في مكان آخر، فنبني النسخة التي تناسب السوق هنا، ننشرها، ونكمل مع ما يستخدمه الناس فعلاً. العربية أولاً، بيانات حقيقية، والتفاصيل التي يتجاوزها الآخرون.
>
> وإلى جانب ذلك نعمل مع الشركات على المنتج والتصميم والتطوير والهوية. المعيار واحد في الحالتين.

> أن نبني برمجيات يرغب الناس هنا في استخدامها فعلاً، وأن نرفع مستوى ما يُبنى في المملكة.

**Vision**

> شركة منتجات سعودية معروفة ببرمجيات استثنائية، منتجاتها تُستخدم كل يوم، وأعمالها تضع المعيار محلياً.
