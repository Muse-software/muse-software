# Brand Identity

Settled. The identity is [[Brand Guideline PDF]] v0.02, 2026, Riyadh. Orange on maroon. It is finished and it is what gets applied to the website, the social accounts and everything else.

The dark glassmorphism and gold, silver and bronze metallic direction from the original strategy dump is dropped. It was an earlier idea that predates the produced brand, and holding both was the single biggest source of confusion in this vault. Recorded in [[Decision Log]].

Product identities are a separate question. A venture may get its own look, sitting under the Muse name without inheriting the maroon. Decide that per product, at [[Venture Pipeline#Shape]], not now.

## The mark

Ascending stepped blocks that form an M. Rounded terminals. The deck reads it as growth and forward movement, and the rounded edges as a smooth experience.

![[logos-01.svg]]

Files live in `99 Meta/Attachments/Brand`. Eight variants exported, horizontal and vertical, in the orange, white and dark treatments.

| Variant | When |
|---|---|
| Horizontal | Default. Anywhere with more width than height |
| Vertical | Square and tall spaces, avatars, stamps |
| Icon only | App icons, favicons, social avatars, small sizes where the wordmark would be illegible |

For the social accounts, the round avatar takes the icon or vertical lockup, never the horizontal one squeezed in. See [[Channels#Activation checklist]].

## Usage rules from the deck

Allowed:

- Orange mark on a clear or dark background
- White mark on the orange background
- White mark on black
- Match orientation to the space. Vertical space gets the vertical lockup

Not allowed:

- Distorting proportions
- Drop shadows
- Recolouring outside the approved treatments
- Shrinking below legible size

## Typography

Space Grotesk for English, IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic. Details, weights and the paste ready tokens are in [[Brand Colour & Type]].

Both are open source and free for commercial use. Space Grotesk gives the English a bit of character without being a display face, and the IBM Plex Arabic is genuinely good rather than an afterthought, which is a real advantage for a brand that has to work in both scripts, see [[Localization Playbook]].

The English face changed from IBM Plex Sans on 2026-07-30, see [[Decision Log]]. Anything produced before that date is on the old face.

## Colour

Full values and mix ratios in [[Brand Colour & Type]]. Short version: deep maroon carries the surface, orange carries the energy, everything else is seasoning.

## Graphic system

The deck uses a thin crosshair grid pattern as its recurring texture, at low contrast over both the orange and the maroon. It is the cheapest way to make a layout feel like ours. SVG elements are in `99 Meta/Attachments/Brand` and in the `Export/SVG` folder outside the vault.
