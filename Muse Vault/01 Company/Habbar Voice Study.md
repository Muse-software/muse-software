# Habbar Voice Study

A read of habbar.com in both locales, done 2026-08-01 because Abdullah likes how they write and wants the mechanics without the positioning. Pages read verbatim: home, services, about-us, firasah, careers, in `ar` and `en`.

Habbar is a Riyadh advertising and creative agency. We are not. Nothing here changes [[Positioning]] or [[Business Lines]]. This note is about sentence construction only.

## The one structural finding

Their Arabic is not one register. It is two, split by page function, and the split is consistent enough to be deliberate.

**MSA spine.** Section headings, service names, the numbered process, anything that has to look like a company: التخطيط الإستراتيجي، الحملات الإبداعية، الإعداد والتخطيط، التسليم وإعداد التقارير ونقل المعرفة. No colloquial markers at all.

**Saudi spoken in the body.** The paragraph under the MSA heading switches: عشان، وش، مو، اللي، دايم، يجينا، تضرب.

Both appear on the same page, a few hundred pixels apart. From `ar/services`:

> نبحث ونقرأ سلوك الناس عشان نعرف وش يحرّكهم فعلًا، مو وش المفروض يحرّكهم

and directly below it, step 01:

> نبدأ بدراسة معمقة لبيئة المشروع، ونراجع سياق المجال والوثائق والمراجع لضمان وضوح الرؤية

That is the whole trick. The MSA carries the credibility, the spoken register carries the personality, and neither has to do the other's job. It is why the site sounds like people without sounding unserious.

**Adopted 2026-08-01.** Our Arabic now runs the same split. [[Arabic Termbase#The register, decided]] carries the rule and the marker list, and [[Decision Log]] carries the reasoning.

## Four mechanics worth taking

### 1. The correction pivot

State the thing, then define it against the near miss that a competitor would have settled for. `X، مو Y`.

| Arabic | English |
|---|---|
| عشان نعرف وش يحرّكهم فعلًا، مو وش المفروض يحرّكهم | to understand what truly drives them, not what we think should drive them |
| تبدأ من السؤال مو من الجواب | |
| ما نعدّ ما أنجزناه.. نشوف ما تركناه | we don't count what we've done; we look at what we've left behind |
| القص عندك مو بس تقني؛ القص عندك سردي | We don't break boundaries; we expand them |
| | we deliver what they need, not just what they asked for |
| | Scoring a goal is great, but scoring it in the wrong net is a failure |

This is the highest value steal and it is positioning agnostic. It also does the [[Website Voice#The specificity test]] job automatically: naming the thing you are not doing forces a real distinction into the sentence.

**The trap.** This shape is one character away from the AI tell that the `humanizer` skill flags, the empty "it's not just X, it's Y". The test that separates them: is the rejected half something a real competitor actually does? "not what we think should drive them" describes real agency behaviour, so it lands. "not just a tool, a revolution" rejects nothing, so it is filler in a confident costume. If the Y half is not a thing someone actually does, cut the whole construction.

### 2. The verb chain

Stacked first person plural verbs, comma chained, closed by a purpose clause on حتى or بهدف or بحيث.

> لأن هدفنا من هدف عميلنا نبحث، ونفكّر، ونجرب، ونختبر، ونصقل، ونعدّل حتى نوصل لفكرة أثرها يدوم سنين.

> we research, ideate, experiment, test, and refine until we land on an idea with an impact that lasts for years

> نسلّم العمل بملفاته الكاملة، ونقيس أثره على أرض الواقع، ونوثّق ما تعلّمناه في تقرير يشرح الأسباب والنتائج

The chain is doing something a list of short sentences cannot: it makes the process feel like one continuous motion rather than a set of stages. That is the argument for allowing it despite [[Brand Voice#Principles, both registers]] saying a sentence needing a comma probably needs a full stop.

Bounded use only. One chain per section, as the rhythmic beat, never as the default sentence shape. The rule stays; this is a named exception to it.

### 3. Verb CTAs bound to the service

Every service card ends with an imperative verb plus the brand, and the verb is different each time:

خطّط مع حبّار / أثّر مع حبّار / صمّم مع حبّار / أنتج مع حبّار
Plan with Habbar / Create Impact with Habbar / Design with Habbar / Produce with Habbar

Also اشتغل معنا and شوفها كلها, both spoken register, both one word longer than they need to be and better for it.

This is [[Website Voice#Applied]] point 4 executed properly. We already have the rule and are not getting this much out of it. The variation is the point: a repeated "Get in touch" under four cards tells the reader the four cards are the same.

### 4. Second person as portrait

Their careers copy describes the person rather than listing requirements:

> تبدأ من السؤال مو من الجواب. تتفرّس في البريف، تلاقي اللي ما انكتب، وتحوّل أهداف العميل إلى أفكار تضرب عبر كل المنصات.

> We see talent before we see the CV. We look for passion, curiosity, and depth, because skills can be built.

Directly usable for [[Hiring]], and the second person verb form is already our house address per [[Arabic Termbase#The register, decided]].

## What to leave

### The classical vocabulary layer

Abdullah's objection to نتفرّس is the right call and it generalises. The tell is that they had to publish a dictionary definition of الفِراسة on its own page to make their own philosophy legible. A word that needs a glossary is a liability on a page that has one scroll to persuade a stranger.

Everything in this layer stays out: نتفرّس، الفِراسة، الاحتبارية (coined from حبر)، الصومعة (as the name of a review panel)، السبعين عذرًا (an allusion requiring the athar to decode)، and lines like أن تفتّش عن الوجه المختبئ خلف قناع المشكلات.

The distinction that matters: **colloquial is not the same as literary.** عشان and وش and مو are plain and everyone reads them instantly. نتفرّس is rare and makes the reader work. Habbar uses both and only the first one is transferable.

### The agency positioning

Not ours, and mostly already blocked by [[Website Voice#We do not claim what is not decided]]:

- A named philosophy with its own page, symbol and CEO quote. Agencies sell taste, so they need a myth. We sell things that run, so we show the thing
- Vanity counters, 10+ / 500+ / 30+ / 1000+
- Self coronation, الوكالة الوطنية للدعاية والإعلان
- Awards as a stated deliverable, بهدف اقتناص فرص الجوائز الممكنة للمشروع. That is agency economics
- Client names, campaign names, logos

## The English is the weaker half

Worth knowing before modelling anything on it. The Arabic is written; the English is translated, and it shows.

- "Where Creativity Meets Objective" does not work as English. Objective is a bare noun where the reader expects "its objective" or "purpose"
- An Arabic comma has leaked into the English footer on every page: "an idea that hasn't found a home، this is the place"
- Process step 03 on `en/services` is wrong copy. Under "Delivery, Reporting & Knowledge Transfer" it describes preparation, duplicating step 01. The Arabic step 03 is correct, so the English is stale
- `ar` home has a broken word, "اعرف المز يد" for المزيد
- They use an em dash in "what truly drives them—not what we think", against our [[Brand Voice#Punctuation house rule]]. No change on our side, just do not copy it

Take the Arabic mechanics and build the English from them natively, which is the [[Brand Voice#Arabic voice]] rule run in the other direction.

## Where this collides with our decided rules

All resolved 2026-08-01, same day as the read. Nothing here is left open.

| Our rule | Habbar | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| [[Arabic Termbase]], one MSA register, not slang | Two registers split by page function | **Adopted.** Split register, marker list in [[Arabic Termbase#The register, decided]] |
| Short sentences, no comma chains | The verb chain is their signature | **Named exception**, one per section, [[Brand Voice#The verb chain]] |
| `humanizer` flags negative parallelism | The correction pivot is that shape done well | **Taken**, gated on the real competitor test, [[Brand Voice#The correction pivot]] |
| Same CTA under every card | A different verb per service | **Adopted**, [[Website Voice#Applied]] point 4 |
| No em dashes | They use one in English | No change, we keep ours |
| Prove, do not claim | Stats, awards, self coronation | No change, already blocked |

The English register was left alone deliberately. It is the weaker half of their site, ours is not bad, and the two languages are written natively rather than in parallel, so the Arabic gaining a spoken layer does not license the English to get a casual one. English takes the two devices and nothing else.

## What this leaves outstanding

Corrected 2026-08-01 after reading the actual copy. The earlier version of this section said the whole of `messages/ar.json` needed a spoken pass. That was wrong, and the reason is worth recording.

The Arabic was already split, just not by page function. It was split **by author**. The homepage is Abdullah's own copy and was already spoken, وش and بس and مو and احنا throughout. Everything else was the drafted MSA. So the site had accidentally arrived at half the rule before the rule existed, which is a decent sign the rule is right.

Done on 2026-08-01: the homepage errors, `/about` in both locales, the careers heroes and role blurbs, and the per service CTA verbs.

Still MSA and still to do: the `/explore`, `/newsletter`, `/contact` and `/get-started` subtitles, and the `intro` and `approachIntro` paragraphs in `lib/content/ar/services.ts`. That copy is strong MSA rather than broken, so under the density rule most of it needs one or two markers, not a rewrite. It still has to happen before the native review in [[Brand Voice#Arabic voice]], not after, or the reviewer checks a moving target.
