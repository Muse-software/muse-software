# V-003 Walking Game

Turn walking into something you want to do, using story and game mechanics rather than step counts. Reference: zombie chase style running apps.

## Problem

Everyone knows they should walk more. Step counters tell you that you did not. The insight in the reference product is that a story gives you a reason to leave the house, and the exercise becomes a side effect.

## Why the reference works

Audio narrative, episodic structure, and a chase mechanic that makes pace meaningful. The user is not exercising, they are finding out what happens next.

## The local gap and the local problem

Honest assessment: this is the weakest of the three on feasibility and the strongest on originality.

Working in favour:

- Walking is the most accessible form of exercise here, and outdoor walking culture in Riyadh has grown with new parks and pedestrian projects
- Weather forces walking into early morning and evening windows, which is a natural session shape
- Content in Arabic, produced for this audience, would be genuinely new. Nothing like it exists
- Mall walking is a real behaviour, and it is indoors and air conditioned

Working against:

- Audio drama is expensive. Writing, voice acting, sound design, and it needs to be good or nobody finishes episode one
- Content is consumable. Users finish it and leave, so retention depends on a production pipeline, not a one time build
- Six months of the year the outdoor window is narrow

## Angles worth considering instead of a straight adaptation

- Family or group mechanic rather than solo. Shared goals travel further culturally than individual streaks
- Corporate wellness as the buyer. Companies pay per seat, which solves distribution and revenue at once, and the content cost is amortised across a contract
- Location aware content tied to real places, parks, corniches, heritage sites, so the walk has a destination
- Original Saudi story world rather than importing a zombie premise
