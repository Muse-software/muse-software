# Idea Scoring

A half hour filter, not a research project. The point is to make the weak spot of an idea visible before anyone falls in love with it.

Deliberately light, because [[Product Philosophy#Not a research project]] says the market decides, not a scoring sheet. This exists to stop obviously bad attempts, not to predict winners.

## Six questions

Answer each yes, maybe or no, in writing, in one or two lines.

| # | Question | What a no means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Is it proven elsewhere?** A mature product with real users and real revenue | We are inventing, which is a different and more expensive game |
| 2 | **Is the local gap real?** Nothing decent exists here, or what exists is clearly not built for here | If a strong Arabic first competitor is already winning, the idea changes |
| 3 | **Can we ship a real v1 with the people we have?** In roughly two months | Park it until the team is different |
| 4 | **Do we know how the first hundred people find it?** Specifically, not "marketing" | Hard stop, see below |
| 5 | **Does localisation change the product,** not just the language? | Hard stop, see below |
| 6 | **Is the problem naturally recurring?** Daily or weekly, by its nature | Hard stop, see below |

## The three hard stops

A no on any of these kills the idea regardless of how good the rest looks.

**Question 4, path to first users.** A product nobody finds is a hobby, and under this model the attempt fails from invisibility rather than from being wrong, which teaches us nothing. This is the one most likely to be wishful thinking, so be hard on it. See [[Influence Strategy#2. Distribution for our own products]].

**Question 5, localisation depth.** If the only change is translation, the original is one update away from taking the market back. The version worth building is the one where local behaviour reshapes the product, see [[Product Philosophy#Localisation is the product, not a translation layer]].

**Question 6, retention shape.** Retention is a property of the problem, not of the design. A genuinely recurring problem retains. One that is not cannot be fixed with notifications.

## Also worth writing down, but not a gate

- How it would make money eventually, even roughly
- Whether it reinforces another venture or the brand, see [[Ventures#Wedge or spread]]
- The one or two additions that make it ours rather than a copy
