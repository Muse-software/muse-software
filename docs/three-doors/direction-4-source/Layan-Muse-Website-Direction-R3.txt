01
The recommended direction
Lead with understanding. Route by intent. Support with
capabilities. Prove with real things.
The website opens with a clear, warm statement of what Muse is and what it believes: that good technology
begins with understanding people, their needs, and their businesses. It then asks the only question that
matters to a visitor, «how can we help?», and routes them by what they are trying to achieve. Everything
after that is support: the capabilities Muse brings together, how the team thinks and works, and the real
things it has made. The site converts by starting the right conversation, not by selling a package.
The tone throughout is human, intelligent, and quietly confident. Nothing argues with the industry,
announces confidence, or asks to be judged. A potential client should feel «they understand what I’m
trying to achieve» before anything else.
Intents route
→
Visitors enter through their goal: bring a new
idea to life, improve an existing product,
explore AI Transformation. Intents own the
homepage and the start flow.
Capabilities combine
→
Strategy & discovery, design, engineering, AI,
gamification & experience. They live in one
homepage band, the navigation, and their
own pages. They never route; they appear
inside the intents they serve.
Approach, not framework
→
No invented «Muse Standard» branding. The
beliefs behind it become a plain, warm «Our
approach» section. If a working checklist
ever emerges from real client work,
publishing it then will be earned.
Real things only
→
Credibility comes from what anyone can
open: experiments, products, writing, and
this site itself. The section is built as slots
that client work naturally fills later.

Why this direction
The five insights that actually shaped the site, each tied to the decision it produced.
Visitors arrive with a goal, not a service taxonomy.
→
Intent-led routing («how can we help?»)
replaces service menus and the earlier Build /
Ventures / Think doors, which mirrored
Muse’s structure instead of the buyer’s.
A new visitor gives the hero about five seconds.
→
The hero must say what Muse is and hint
who it’s for at a glance. Clever riddle
headlines and proof-style claims both fail
that test; clarity with a warm point of view
passes it.
Muse is new, with limited public proof.
→
Credibility through real artifacts and clarity,
not claims: live experiments and products,
published thinking, and a site that
demonstrates the craft it sells. No borrowed
metrics, no logo walls.
The brand needs warmth and intelligence, not
confrontation.
→
A human voice throughout, and «Our
approach» instead of an invented authority
framework. Nothing anti-agency, nothing
defensive, no announcing how different
Muse is.
Two different readers shop two different ways.
→
Problem-holders route by situation;
evaluators and referrals look for named skills.
So the architecture has two layers: intents
on the homepage, capabilities in the
navigation and on their own pages.

netguru.com

Musemind
musemind.agency
Nearly every surface opens with the buyer’s pain in the

Local operational legitimacy stated concretely: a Saudi

Hero: three territories
TERRITORY A · UNDERSTANDING FIRST · RECOMMENDED
Great products begin with understanding.
Muse is a product studio in Riyadh. We bring together strategy, design, engineering and AI to turn ideas
and challenges into digital products made for real people.
[ Start a conversation ]
The thoughtful partner: the belief leads, the support line answers what and where Muse is. Headline and support must
always appear together, and the rest of the page has to make «understanding» true rather than decorative.
TERRITORY B · THE CLEAR INTRODUCTION
Thoughtful digital products, designed and built in Riyadh.
From new ideas to products already out in the world, Muse combines product thinking, design,
engineering and AI to make technology feel considered and human.
[ Start a conversation ]
The calm specialist: the headline alone passes the five-second test. The safe fallback if A reads too abstract in testing.
TERRITORY C · MADE TOGETHER
Let’s make something people will love using.
Muse is a small multidisciplinary studio in Riyadh: product thinkers, designers and engineers who work
directly with you, from first idea to living product.
[ Start a conversation ]
The invitation: collaboration and smallness as strengths. Whichever hero wins, this support line deserves a home on the
site.
Recommendation: Territory A. It expresses the emotional center of the direction, and «how can we help?»
follows it naturally. Sanity-check all three with a few cold readers on real mockups; design does not wait for
the result.

SECTION 2 · HOW CAN WE HELP?
How can we help?
Every project starts somewhere different. Where are you today?
Bring a new idea to life. You have an idea, a plan, or a problem worth solving. We help you shape it, design
it, and build it into a product people can actually use. → Start something new
Make an existing product better. You already have a product, and you sense it could be more. We help you
understand what’s holding it back, then improve it thoughtfully, or rebuild the parts that need it. →
Improve a product
Explore AI for your business. AI is full of promise, and full of noise. We help you find where it genuinely
helps your business and your customers, then build it into how you work. → Explore AI Transformation
Looking for a specific capability? See what we bring together below.
SECTION 3 · WHAT WE BRING TOGETHER
What we bring together
One team, five disciplines. Most projects draw on several at once.
Product Strategy & Discovery. Understanding your users, your business, and the problem itself, so we
build the right thing before we build anything.
Product & Experience Design. Interfaces and experiences that feel clear, considered, and natural to use, in
Arabic and English alike.
Product Engineering. Reliable, well-crafted software, built by senior engineers who stay close to the
product and to you.
AI. Practical intelligence inside products and workflows: features and systems that quietly make things
easier for the people using them.
Gamification & Experience. The craft of motivation. We design the moments, feedback, and rewards that
help people form real habits with a product, instead of trying it once and drifting away.

Homepage copy: approach, why Muse, from the studio
SECTION 4 · OUR APPROACH
Our approach
A few beliefs shape everything we make.
Start with people. We spend time understanding your users and your business before we design
anything. Good decisions come from context, not templates.
Design and engineering, one conversation. The people who design your product and the people who build
it work side by side, so ideas survive the journey from sketch to software.
Show, early and often. You’ll see real progress from the first weeks, and you’ll always be talking directly
with the people doing the work.
Care for the quiet details. An empty screen, an error message, a first-time experience: the small moments
are where products earn trust, so we design them with the same care as the big ones.
SECTION 5 · WHY MUSE
Why Muse
We’re a small, multidisciplinary Saudi team: product thinkers, designers, and engineers who genuinely
enjoy building together. We understand this market, its people, and the way products need to feel here,
because it’s home.
Being small is deliberate. It means design and technology live under one roof, specialist depth in AI and
engagement design sits in the same room as the engineers, and the people you talk to are the people
shaping and building your product.
SECTION 6 · FROM THE STUDIO
From the studio
We like making things, for our clients and for ourselves. Our own products and experiments are where we
test ideas, sharpen our craft, and explore what’s next. You’re welcome to look around.
Cards, each linking to something live: Muse Experiments (open source) · Tenet (early access) · Notes (our
writing) · How we built this site. A grid of slots, so client work takes its place here naturally as it becomes
real and shareable.

Homepage copy: FAQ + final ask
SECTION 7 · QUESTIONS PEOPLE ASK US
What kinds of projects do you take on? New products from the first idea, existing products that need
care or a rethink, and focused work in design, engineering, AI, or engagement. Our clients range from
founders to established organizations.
Who will we actually be working with? A small senior team. The people you meet in the first conversation
are the people who will design and build your product.
How do projects usually start? With a conversation. We listen, ask a lot of questions, and take time to
understand what you’re trying to achieve. If we believe we can genuinely help, we come back with a clear
picture of how we’d approach it.
What does AI Transformation mean at Muse? Finding where AI genuinely helps your business, your
operations, your customers, or your product, and building it in thoughtfully. We care most about what
your team will actually use day to day.
What is Gamification & Experience? The psychology of engagement, applied with care. New tools compete
with old habits, so we design the onboarding, feedback, and reward systems that help a change stick, in
products, learning, and workplaces.
SECTION 8 · START A CONVERSATION
Tell us what you’re working on.
We’d love to hear about it. Your first conversation is with the people who would design and build it.

Open decisions + next step
None of these block design.
Hero territory. A is recommended; sanity-check A, B, and C with a few cold readers on real mockups.
Intent card labels. «Bring a new idea to life» / «Make an existing product better» / «Explore AI for your
business» are working labels; confirm final wording alongside the hero check.
Team identity. Named, anonymous, or no names on the site with full identity in the first call. Only /about
and one FAQ answer are affected; the copy already works for all three.
«From the studio» at launch. Confirm which experiments and products are genuinely ready to be looked
at; the section only works if everything in it is real and live.
Capability naming. «AI» vs. «AI Engineering» as the capability label; the intent page stays «AI
Transformation» either way.
Arabic. Written separately, from scratch, as Arabic, once this English direction is locked. Not a translation
pass.
Next step. Align on this deck in the working session, then design begins from it directly. The full
MUSE-DIR walkthrough (section-exact captures, verbatim copy, evidence) is produced once the
hero and label checks are done.
