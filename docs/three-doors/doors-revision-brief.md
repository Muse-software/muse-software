## 2. Non-negotiable rules (vault, always apply)

Source: Muse Vault/01 Company/Website Voice.md + Brand Voice.md + Deck Copy v0.03.md

- Register: direct and confident. An operator talking, not a consultant presenting.
- Second person. Headlines carry the argument, body carries the proof.
- Specificity test: any sentence a competitor could publish unchanged is filler. Cut or
  rewrite it into something only Muse can say.
- No inflation: not leading/premier/cutting-edge anything.
- We do not claim what is not decided: no client names/logos/results, no team size, no
  pricing model, no publishing history. PIF experience is stated as experience, not clients.
- Punctuation house rule: straight quotes only. NO em dashes, NO en dashes, no guillemets.
- Banned phrases: innovative solutions, cutting edge technology, disrupting, seamless,
  synergy, leveraging, empowering, unlocking, passionate about, best in class, next level,
  thrilled/excited to announce, "X، لا Y" AI-slop pattern (use "X، مو Y" / "X, not Y" pivot
  only when the rejected half is something a competitor actually does).
- Correction pivot device (X not Y) and verb chain (once per section) are allowed, from the
  Habbar study.
- The pillars (Ventures, Marketing, Studio) are INTERNAL language, never public. The doors
  are the public face of the business lines: Build = client work, Ventures = own products,
  Think = knowledge & community.
- Arabic is written natively in parallel, never a translation of English. MSA spine for
  headings/service names; Saudi spoken for body/CTAs (عشان وش مو اللي بس, 1-2 markers per
  paragraph max). No classical vocabulary, no deep dialect (تبي/أبغى banned).
- Arabic RTL: everything must render correctly, nothing is "shown, not claimed".

## 4. Research references (all verbatim, verified live Aug 5, 2026)

### 4a. doors-ref-global.md (66 quotes: tenex, 37signals, basecamp, vercel, linear, netlify)
Key mechanics:
- NO reference site uses a per-card eyebrow. Tenex/Netlify: one per section. Linear and
  Basecamp: none at all. Verdict: one small eyebrow per door section or none.
- Balanced card skeleton: 1-4 word title -> one-liner 6-14 words -> one CTA. Description
  word counts within ~2x across the trio (Linear 12-14, Netlify 10-14, Tenex 16-23).
- Titles as verbs, not nouns: "Define the product direction", "Move work forward", "Go live
  everywhere". Doors read as actions (ways to work), not departments.
- One-liner formulas: "From X to Y", "X not Y", "No X. No Y.", "Every X gets a Y".
- Proof lives in the one-liner: Tenex Engineering ends "you pay for features delivered, not
  hours logged."
- CTA mechanics: identical CTA on every card (system feel) OR distinct verb CTA per card
  (each door its own offer). Never generic "Get in touch" repeated.
- 37signals is the cleanest Think-door model: knowledge as a numbered list of punchy essay
  titles ("Signal 01. An obligation to independence"), zero descriptions needed.
- Vercel "Recently shipped": 1-word title + 6-word one-liner, whole card is a link.

### 4b. doors-ref-saudi.md (75 quotes: salla, mozn, zto, lean, thmanyah)
Key mechanics:
- Salla: "Your store. Your brand. Your future." trio framing; 3-line card rhythm
  (title 1-3 words / subtitle 3-6 words / body 8-20 words).
- Mozn: "Our engineers work inside your environment, not from a distance... proving value
  in weeks, not quarters." (X not Y contrast); "weeks, not quarters" is directly adaptable.
- ZTO: contrastive positioning "adds to your credibility rather than dilute it"; "not the
  other way around" distinguishes own values from client work.
- ZTO editorial independence paragraph: the strongest example of a company distinguishing
  its own product/values from client-facing work.
- Bilingual parallelism with shared metrics (ZTO "100K decision-makers" in EN+AR at same
  grid position) signals one brand, not a translation.
- Thmanyah: four content lanes as equal nav items (المقالات / الإذاعة / الوثائقيات /
  النشرات البريدية).

### 4c. doors-ref-bigtech.md (31 quotes: stripe, shopify, notion, framer)
Key mechanics:
- One grammar skeleton per row, zero eyebrows. Title (2-5 words) + one-liner (~5-15 words).
- Length balance enforced by sentence count, not word count.
- Verb-first distinctions: Shopify six cards on one verb "Sell..." differentiated by object;
  Framer "Design with an agent / Code with an agent"; Notion one-liners start with verbs.
- One recurring CTA verb per set, one deliberate exception.
- Two-fragment payoff lines: "Less tracking. More progress." (Notion), "Manage more.
  Publish faster." (Framer). Reads designed, not transcribed.

### 4d. ui-patterns-global.md + ui-patterns-saudi.md (IN PROGRESS — 2 agents running)

details (hover motion, border treatment, typography tricks, asymmetric grids, RTL moves).
When they land, read and fold into the plan.
