Direction 3: The Proof

The Proof is a work-led homepage: it opens with what Muse has actually shipped, not a thesis or a pitch, then opens the three doors as ways to act on that proof. A work-led homepage direction for muse.sa. The site opens with what Muse has actually shipped rather than a thesis, then opens Build, Ventures and Think as three ways to act on that proof. Screenshots in this walkthrough are section-exact captures from the direction build, taken at 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 mobile, both at a 2x device scale. Copy is quoted verbatim from the site's own content files.

One of three homepage directions, each built and deployed separately. This walkthrough documents the Proof direction section by section, in the exact order it renders on the page, in both languages, discloses where the Arabic route currently renders English copy, and ends with the decisions the team needs to make before this direction ships.

FAQ · content and rationale

Answers five real objections a serious buyer raises before committing, in the buyer's own language, on the page, rather than making them ask.

Content anatomy
  • Section heading.
  • Five accordion rows, first one open by default.
  • Each row: a question in the buyer's voice, a direct answer with no hedging.
Verbatim copy
English

Heading: Questions? We have answers.

How is Muse different from a typical dev shop?: We're a small, senior team, not a rotating cast of contractors. No 100-slide strategy decks with nothing behind them. We scope fast, build with you, and measure ourselves on what actually shipped.

What does AI transformation actually mean?: Finding where AI removes real friction in how your business runs, building the systems that do it reliably, and getting your team actually using them. Then repeating that cycle as the tools improve.

Who's on the team?: A small senior team based in Riyadh. The people who scope your work are the people who build it, so you're talking to engineers in the first conversation rather than a sales layer. Names and backgrounds are on the about page.

How does pricing work?: Every engagement is scoped and priced on its own, once we understand what you're actually trying to do. Tell us the problem and we'll come back with a number and exactly what sits inside it.

Who do you typically work with?: Teams that want to move fast without dropping the quality bar. That runs from a founder shipping a first product to an enterprise team trying to get an AI initiative out of pilot purgatory.

العربية

العنوان: أكيد عندك أسئلة.

وش يفرقكم عن شركات التطوير الثانية؟: احنا فريق صغير من أصحاب الخبرة، مو مجموعة متعاقدين جايين عن طريق وسيط. ما راح نسوي لك عرض استراتيجي من 100 شريحة ما أحد يفهمه. بنجلس معك، ونفهم منك، ثم نحدّد النطاق ونبني معك. هدفنا مو التسليم، هدفنا نبني منتجات نفخر فيها، جودتها عالية وتخدم احتياجك فعلًا.

ما معنى التحوّل بالذكاء الاصطناعي عمليًا؟: الهدف مو أن كل موظفينك يستخدمون الذكاء الاصطناعي. الهدف أننا نفهم وين المكان اللي نقدر نوظّفه فيه عشان يقدّم فائدة فعلية قابلة للقياس، تحدث أثرًا في طريقة عمل شركتك. وإن احتجنا، نبني الأنظمة اللي تمكّن فريقك فعليًا على أرض الواقع. ثم نقيس الأثر ونحسّن بناءً على الاستخدام الفعلي، مو على التخيلات.

مين في الفريق؟: فريق صغير من أصحاب الخبرة، مقرّه الرياض. واللي يجلس معك ويحدّد نطاق عملك ويجتمع معك بشكل دوري هو نفسه اللي قاعد يشتغل عليه، بدون فريق مبيعات أو وسطاء.

كيف يعمل التسعير؟: كل مشروع يُحدَّد نطاقه ويُسعَّر على حدة، بعد ما نفهم وش تحاول تسوي بالضبط. خبّرنا بالمشكلة، ونرجع لك برقم يوضح وش يشمل.

مع من تعملون بالعادة؟: فرق تريد التحرك بسرعة من غير ما تتنازل عن الجودة. من مؤسّس يطلق منتجه الأول إلى مؤسسات وشركات كبيرة تحاول إخراج مبادرات ذكاء اصطناعي ومنتجات من مراحل تجريبية ونماذج أوّلية مالها نهاية.

Each decision below carries a recommendation. A different answer from the team changes the plan.

1. D3-AR: the Arabic route currently renders English copy on four sections plus shared chrome

On this branch, messages/ar.json stores English strings for Home.hero, every Home.proofWall field and card, CTA, Footer and Nav, and i18n/request.ts performs no runtime fallback between locales. The Arabic route renders genuine RTL layout, but the words themselves are English in the Hero, the Proof Wall, the Final CTA, the Footer and the shared navigation chrome. Doors, Manifesto, Outcomes, Ticker and FAQ are genuinely Arabic.

Localize Home.hero, Home.proofWall, CTA, Footer and Nav into native Arabic before this direction is circulated externally. Until then, ship the walkthrough with the honest RTL and English-catalogue label used throughout this document; do not fabricate Arabic to make a screenshot look complete.

2. D3-PROOF: the Proof Wall's quantitative claims are not independently substantiated

The Proof Wall states 94 percent accuracy against a human baseline, a 40 percent retention lift, and a PIF-level financial institution client, none of which carry a supporting evidence page, source citation or client name in the current build.

Add evidence pages that substantiate each figure, or soften or anonymize the claims further before external circulation. This walkthrough labels them as current website claims pending substantiation, not as verified results, and that label should carry over to any external-facing version of this direction.
