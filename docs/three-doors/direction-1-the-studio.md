# Direction 1: The Studio

## Creative direction

The Studio is a portfolio-first, work-led website. It leads with what Muse does (build things, help others build things, share knowledge) and lets the visitor pick the door that matches them. The homepage is one scroll: thesis → three doors → experience → proof → CTA.

## Homepage section order

1. **Hero** — "We're a product team in Riyadh. We actually build the thing, not just the deck about the thing." Keep the existing hero structure (dither background, tagline, subtitle, CTA).
2. **Three Doors** — Build / Ventures / Think. Each is a card with a checkable promise and a CTA.
3. **Experience signal** — "Before Muse, I spent years shipping enterprise applications at scale." Honest, no names, no logos. Lives on About page, not homepage.
4. **Proof wall** — Anonymized case studies, venture pipeline, content that's been shipped. Starts thin, grows.
5. **FAQ** — Specific, checkable answers. No generic consultant talk.
6. **CTA** — "Tell us what you're trying to build." Intent-split: "Are you building a product?" vs "Do you need a team?"

## Three doors

### Build
What we do for clients: production-grade software, shippable AI features, real interfaces. Built fast, stakeholder-friendly, quality UX.

### Ventures
We're building our own products too. The golden egg hunt. Open to collaborating with people who have ideas. Real pipeline, real progress.

### Think
Knowledge sharing, articles, tutorials, showcases, newsletter. Building a name in the Saudi startup/AI/ventures community. Not just sharing knowledge — building influence and brand.

## Service pages (reframed)

Instead of "AI Transformation" (consultant framing), the three service pages are:

1. **Product Engineering** — Production-grade software, shipped fast, without dropping the quality bar.
2. **AI Integration** — Shippable AI features that are actually useful, not strategy decks.
3. **Brand & Interface** — Real interfaces with real UX, not pretty pictures.

## Content approach

- Study global references (Tenex, ZTO, Lean, Salla) for English copy mechanics
- Adapt proven sections, not copy lines
- No AI-generated copy — all human-reviewed
- English first, Arabic later with native writer
- Anonymized case studies ready for Phase 2

## Key differences from current site

- No "AI-absent to AI-native" headline (Tenex copy)
- No "AI Transformation" as a service name
- Three doors instead of three generic service cards
- Proof wall instead of empty "trusted by" section
- Experience signal on About, not homepage
- FAQ answers are specific to Muse, not generic consultant talk
