Revision 2 (2026-08-05)

The hero is now thesis-specific ("Shipping is easy now. / Shipping well isn't.") instead of the shared line, the doors were rebuilt without eyebrows, the problem-card emoji were removed, and the em-dash spans in the standard copy became full stops, in both languages.

Open decisions

Five decisions remain. Each carries a recommendation; a different answer from the team changes the plan.

1. The problem section names real market failures

It calls out content farms and fake case studies by name, which is the point of the direction. It is also the riskiest tone on the site. Recommendation: keep it; it is what makes this direction different from a polite company site.

2. The PIF reference on the homepage

The standard section states the founder's enterprise experience on the homepage. The other directions keep that signal on the About page only. Recommendation: keep it here, because it is the stated reason the standard exists; keep it off the homepage in the other directions.

3. Em dashes in the standard copy: RESOLVED (rev 2)

The three em-dash spans in the standard copy were rewritten as full stops in revision 2 ("The difference isn't talent. It's whether you're willing to do the work that nobody sees" and the two other paragraphs), matching the site's punctuation rule, in both languages.

4. Icon treatment in the problem cards: RESOLVED (rev 2)

The three emoji icons were removed entirely in revision 2, matching the no-icon treatment used by Linear and Vercel. The cards are title and body only.

5. Doors identical across directions

The three doors carry the same copy in every direction, only their position changes. Recommendation: keep them identical; the doors are the business model, not the message.
