Direction 2: The Thesis

The Thesis is an argument-led homepage: it names the market problem, states the quality bar Muse holds against it, then opens the three doors as consequences of that standard, not as a service menu. An argument-first homepage direction for muse.sa. The site states its founding claim in the hero, names the problem it is arguing against, states the standard it holds itself to, then opens Build, Ventures and Think as three ways to act on that standard. Screenshots in this walkthrough are section-exact captures from the direction build, taken at 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 mobile, both at a 2x device scale. Copy is quoted verbatim from the site's own content files.

One of three homepage directions, each built and deployed separately. This walkthrough documents the Thesis direction section by section, in the exact order it renders on the page, in both languages, and ends with the decisions the team needs to make before this direction ships.

English

Eyebrow: The problem we see

Heading: Everyone has a strategy. Almost nobody ships.

Item 1 title: AI strategies nobody ships

Item 1 body: Every company has an AI strategy. Almost nobody has an AI feature in production. The gap between deck and reality is where value dies.

Item 2 title: Content farms, not knowledge

Item 2 body: The Saudi AI space is full of reposted threads, 10-tip listicles, and videos claiming Claude built a website that was actually hand-coded. Signal-to-noise is near zero.

Item 3 title: Fake case studies, real budgets

Item 3 body: Agencies show logos they never worked with. Consultants sell transformations they've never executed. Clients pay for decks, not outcomes.

English

Eyebrow: The standard we hold

Heading: Muse is the quality bar. The other choice.

Paragraph 1: We've seen what bad looks like. We know what good looks like. The difference isn't talent. It's whether you're willing to do the work that nobody sees.

Paragraph 2: Real data in every state. Every edge case designed. Nothing ships that we wouldn't use ourselves. That's not a slogan. It's a written checklist we run on every release.

Paragraph 3: The founder spent years shipping enterprise applications at PIF-level organizations. That experience isn't the story. It's the reason the standard exists. Muse is the line.

The three pillars make the quality bar operational. English and Arabic are shown together before the placement and source explanation.

English

Pillar 1 title: Real data, every state

Pillar 1 body: No lorem ipsum in reviews. No happy-path-only designs. Empty states, error states, loading states, all real, all reviewed.

FAQ · content and rationale

Content anatomy
  • Section heading.
  • Five accordion rows, first one open by default.

Verbatim copy
English

Heading: Questions? We have answers.

How is Muse different from a typical dev shop?: We're a small, senior team, not a rotating cast of contractors. No 100-slide strategy decks with nothing behind them. We scope fast, build with you, and measure ourselves on what actually shipped.

What does AI transformation actually mean?: Finding where AI removes real friction in how your business runs, building the systems that do it reliably, and getting your team actually using them. Then repeating that cycle as the tools improve.

Who's on the team?: A small senior team based in Riyadh. The people who scope your work are the people who build it, so you're talking to engineers in the first conversation rather than a sales layer. Names and backgrounds are on the about page.

How does pricing work?: Every engagement is scoped and priced on its own, once we understand what you're actually trying to do. Tell us the problem and we'll come back with a number and exactly what sits inside it.

Who do you typically work with?: Teams that want to move fast without dropping the quality bar. That runs from a founder shipping a first product to an enterprise team trying to get an AI initiative out of pilot purgatory.

العربية

العنوان: أكيد عندك أسئلة.

وش يفرقكم عن شركات التطوير الثانية؟: احنا فريق صغير من أصحاب الخبرة، مو مجموعة متعاقدين جايين عن طريق وسيط. ما راح نسوي لك عرض استراتيجي من 100 شريحة ما أحد يفهمه. بنجلس معك، ونفهم منك، ثم نحدّد النطاق ونبني معك. هدفنا مو التسليم، هدفنا نبني منتجات نفخر فيها، جودتها عالية وتخدم احتياجك فعلًا.

ما معنى التحوّل بالذكاء الاصطناعي عمليًا؟: الهدف مو أن كل موظفينك يستخدمون الذكاء الاصطناعي. الهدف أننا نفهم وين المكان اللي نقدر نوظّفه فيه عشان يقدّم فائدة فعلية قابلة للقياس، تحدث أثرًا في طريقة عمل شركتك. وإن احتجنا، نبني الأنظمة اللي تمكّن فريقك فعليًا على أرض الواقع. ثم نقيس الأثر ونحسّن بناءً على الاستخدام الفعلي، مو على التخيلات.
