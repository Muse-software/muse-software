Muse Site Walkthrough
Direction 1: The Studio

The Studio is a portfolio-first homepage: it leads with what Muse builds, then lets the visitor pick the door that matches them, Build, Ventures, or Think. A portfolio-first homepage direction for muse.sa. The site states the identity in one line, then organizes everything Muse does as three doors: Build, Ventures, Think. Screenshots in this walkthrough are section-exact captures from the direction build, taken at 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 mobile, both at a 2x device scale. Copy is quoted verbatim from the site's own content files.

One of three homepage directions, each built and deployed separately. This walkthrough documents the Studio direction section by section, in the exact order it renders on the page, in both languages, and ends with the decisions the team needs to make before this direction ships.

Manifesto · content and rationale

Makes the why-now argument: the cost of building good software is falling, which means the gap between companies that move and companies that plan is about to become permanent. Closes with the sharpest form of that tension and a second CTA.

Content anatomy
  • Section heading: the five-year claim.
  • Three short paragraphs building the argument in sequence.
  • Pressure statement: two short lines, plain claim then its consequence.
  • CTA, into the same contact flow as the Hero.
Verbatim copy
English

Heading: The next decade of software gets decided in the next two years.

Paragraph 1: The cost of building good software is collapsing. That doesn't help you on its own. It means the gap between the companies that move and the ones that plan is about to get permanent.

Paragraph 2: You could drive that from inside. Most won't, and not for lack of ambition. Day to day operations always win the calendar.

Paragraph 3: We're not interested in your roadmap for this quarter. We're interested in the one that matters in five years.

Pressure, lead: AI isn't optional.

Pressure, emphasis: Waiting is the risk.

CTA: Get started

FAQ · content and rationale

Answers five real objections a serious buyer raises before committing, in the buyer's own language, on the page, rather than making them ask.

Content anatomy
  • Section heading.
  • Five accordion rows, first one open by default.

Verbatim copy
English

Heading: Questions? We have answers.

How is Muse different from a typical dev shop?: We're a small, senior team, not a rotating cast of contractors. No 100-slide strategy decks with nothing behind them. We scope fast, build with you, and measure ourselves on what actually shipped.

What does AI integration actually mean?: Finding where AI removes real friction in how your business runs, building the systems that do it reliably, and getting your team actually using them. Then repeating that cycle as the tools improve.

Who's on the team?: A small senior team based in Riyadh. The people who scope your work are the people who build it, so you're talking to engineers in the first conversation rather than a sales layer. Names and backgrounds are on the about page.

How does pricing work?: Every engagement is scoped and priced on its own, once we understand what you're actually trying to do. Tell us the problem and we'll come back with a number and exactly what sits inside it.

Who do you typically work with?: Teams that want to move fast without dropping the quality bar. That runs from a founder shipping a first product to an enterprise team trying to get an AI initiative out of pilot purgatory.

العربية

العنوان: أكيد عندك أسئلة.

وش يفرقكم عن شركات التطوير الثانية؟: احنا فريق صغير من أصحاب الخبرة، مو مجموعة متعاقدين جايين عن طريق وسيط. ما راح نسوي لك عرض استراتيجي من 100 شريحة ما أحد يفهمه. بنجلس معك، ونفهم منك، ثم نحدّد النطاق ونبني معك. هدفنا مو التسليم، هدفنا نبني منتجات نفخر فيها، جودتها عالية وتخدم احتياجك فعلًا.

ما معنى التحوّل بالذكاء الاصطناعي عمليًا؟: الهدف مو أن كل موظفينك يستخدمون الذكاء الاصطناعي. الهدف أننا نفهم وين المكان اللي نقدر نوظّفه فيه عشان يقدّم فائدة فعلية قابلة للقياس، تحدث أثرًا في طريقة عمل شركتك. وإن احتجنا، نبني الأنظمة اللي تمكّن فريقك فعليًا على أرض الواقع. ثم نقيس الأثر ونحسّن بناءً على الاستخدام الفعلي، مو على التخيلات.

مين في الفريق؟: فريق صغير من أصحاب الخبرة، مقرّه الرياض. واللي يجلس معك ويحدّد نطاق عملك ويجتمع معك بشكل دوري هو نفسه اللي قاعد يشتغل عليه، بدون فريق مبيعات أو وسطاء.

كيف يعمل التسعير؟: كل مشروع يُحدَّد نطاقه ويُسعَّر على حدة، بعد ما نفهم وش تحاول تسوي بالضبط. خبّرنا بالمشكلة، ونرجع لك برقم يوضح وش يشمل.

مع من تعملون بالعادة؟: فرق تريد التحرك بسرعة من غير ما تتنازل عن الجودة. من مؤسّس يطلق منتجه الأول إلى مؤسسات وشركات كبيرة تحاول إخراج مبادرات ذكاء اصطناعي ومنتجات من مراحل تجريبية ونماذج أوّلية مالها نهاية.

Open decisions

Each decision below carries a recommendation. A different answer from the team changes the plan.

1. Doors as navigation, or doors as homepage sections

The three doors sit mid-page and link onward to /build, /ventures and /think. They could instead become the primary site navigation, with /explore retired.

Keep the doors as homepage sections and keep /explore as the deep-dive summary page. A visitor who wants one door gets that door's full page; a visitor who wants all three gets the homepage summary first.

2. The Think door, before its content exists

The Think door promises articles, field notes and a newsletter. The newsletter signup is live; the articles are not published yet.

Ship the door with the newsletter linked, and hold the articles claim until the first two pieces are actually published. A door that links to nothing published yet erodes trust faster than a door that is temporarily thin.

3. The Arabic register

The Doors and FAQ copy is written in a deliberately colloquial Saudi register, while Legal and Careers content stays formal.

Confirm the split with a native copy review before this direction ships externally: colloquial for Hero, Doors, Manifesto and FAQ, formal for Legal and Careers.

4. Unused service entries in the content files

messages/en.json and messages/ar.json carry three additional entries under Home.doors (product-engineering, ai-integration, brand-interface) that ThreeDoors.tsx never reads; only build, ventures and think render.

Remove the three unused entries before merge so the content files match what the site actually shows, or confirm they are intentionally held for a future service-page reuse.

5. Split legibility versus page count

Every section in this direction measured under the mobile and desktop split budgets (1500 CSS px mobile, 1400 CSS px desktop) at capture time, so no section in this walkthrough required a split page.

If a future revision of this direction grows any section past that budget, add a labeled continuation page rather than shrinking a phone screenshot into an unreadable strip. This walkthrough's own capture measurements are recorded in capture-manifest.json for exactly that comparison.
