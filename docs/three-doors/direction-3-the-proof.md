# Direction 3: The Proof

## Creative direction

The Proof is a work-led site that leads with WHAT Muse has shipped. No thesis statement upfront — just the work. The homepage is a curated proof wall: anonymized case studies, live venture experiments, content shipped. The three doors appear as ways to access that work, not as navigation.

## Homepage section order

1. **Hero** — Same visual (dither, statue). Headline: "We don't pitch. We ship." Subtitle: "Everything below is real work. Anonymized case studies. Live experiments. Content we wrote."
2. **Proof Wall** — The main section. Grid of work cards:
   - Enterprise app for PIF-level org (anonymized)
   - AI feature shipped for fintech client
   - Brand interface for consumer product
   - Venture experiment 1 (live)
   - Venture experiment 2 (live)
   - Newsletter issues
3. **Three Doors** — "Want this built for you?" / "Want to build with us?" / "Want to learn how we think?"
4. **Standard Footer** — The quality bar statement (condensed from The Standard section)
5. **CTA** — "Show us what you're building."

## Key differences from Directions 1 & 2

- No thesis, no problem section — just the work
- Hero is minimal, proof-first
- Proof Wall is the bulk of the homepage
- Three doors are CTAs, not navigation
- Experience signal is implicit in the work shown
- Most opinionated toward "show, don't tell"
