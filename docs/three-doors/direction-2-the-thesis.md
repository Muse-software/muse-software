# Direction 2: The Thesis

## Creative direction

The Thesis is a mission-led site that leads with WHY Muse exists. The frustration with slop, the quality bar, the other choice. The homepage thesis is the hero argument — not a consultant pitch, not a service menu. The three doors are still there but presented as consequences of the thesis, not as the primary navigation.

## Homepage section order

1. **Hero** — The founding thesis: "We're sick of slop. We know what good looks like. Muse is the other choice." Same visual (dither, statue), new copy.
2. **The Problem** — What we see in the market: AI strategies nobody ships, content farms, fake case studies, slop that works because there's no alternative.
3. **The Standard** — What Muse holds: real data, every state designed, nothing shipped we wouldn't use ourselves. The quality bar.
4. **Three Doors** — Build / Ventures / Think — as expressions of the standard, not separate services.
5. **Experience Signal** — "Before Muse, I spent years shipping enterprise apps at scale." On About page but referenced here.
6. **Proof Wall** — Anonymized work, venture pipeline, content shipped.
7. **CTA** — "Tell us what you're trying to build."

## Key differences from Direction 1

- Hero leads with thesis/frustration, not "AI strategy" rotating text
- "The Problem" section explicitly calls out the slop
- "The Standard" section defines the quality bar
- Three doors are downstream of the thesis
- Experience signal referenced on homepage (link to About)
- More opinionated, more specific to Muse's founding story
