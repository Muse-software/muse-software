## Key discovery — why the site feels vibe-coded

The English voice Abdullah admires is **tenex.co** — but Tenex is a US/NYC
company (founded by Alex Lieberman of Morning Brew), not Saudi. The Muse main
branch literally copy-pasted its lines: "Win the next decade.", "Your AI
transformation partner.", "We set & execute your enterprise AI strategy at
startup speed.", "AI-absent to AI-native.", "No 6-month diagnostics. No
200-slide presentations.". That is why the old copy reads foreign: it is a New
York company's voice. The staging branch already replaced most of it with
Abdullah's own lines. Keep going in that direction; borrow Tenex's *mechanics*
(short, specific, opinionated, no filler) but not its lines.

## D) CTA discipline (learned from all three)

- Salla: one conversion verb repeated everywhere ("أنشئ متجرك مجانًا"), secondary verbs vary by section outcome ("Build your store today", "Start selling today", "Grow with Salla").
- Tenex: two-tier system — "Learn more" (educate) → "Get Started" (convert), constant everywhere; careers uses "View open roles" / "Apply" / "say hi anyway".
- Lean: one confident verb repeated ("Let's meet!"), one informality beat.
- zto: "اشترك" everywhere; "إذا كنت تبحث عن شريك لتعزيز سردية شركتك وتمييزها في السوق، تواصل معنا" (second-person conditional CTA — great for Muse's contact section).
- Muse already does per-service verb CTAs in AR (خطّط للتحوّل etc.) — keep and extend to EN.
